  no image id, and the endpoint that would (`/v1/images/<id>/`) still 401s. `region_name` (realm only,

  triple, and `global` coverage forbids a source-level centroid.
- **coralscapes** — the paper states site locations are deliberately withheld (anti-poaching); even a
  country-level centroid is both unmappable (no site→country table published) and too wide (Djibouti to
  the Gulf of Aqaba is ≈ 2,200 km).

Largest unlock now: none of the three WP-2d targets has a further legal path — mermaid-aws and
coralscapes are permanently `none` absent an upstream policy change; NOAA is done.
