## Priority

`priority = value(1-5, S57 catalog) / stated-or-measured GB`, labelled/
benchmark formats and smaller sources naturally rank above unlabelled or
larger ones at equal value. wave=w1a is the one documented exception: those
6 rows are pinned to `2000 − rank` (top of the range) per the 2026-09-25
manager directive to rank verified labelled-benchmark data first regardless
of size. The rule is also documented in `_queue.tsv`'s own header comment.
